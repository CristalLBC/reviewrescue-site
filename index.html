What I will never do

This part matters more than the price. Review services have a bad reputation because too many of them break Google's rules and get the client's listing punished for it.

No bought or fake reviews

Every review we reply to was left by a real customer. I never write reviews, never buy them, and never swap them.

No paying for praise

Nothing is ever offered — no discount, no gift, no comp — in exchange for a review. That's against Google's rules and it lands on your listing, not on me.

No cherry-picking

If I ask your past customers for reviews, I ask all of them — not just the happy ones. Selective asking is "review gating" and it's exactly what gets listings penalised.

Not enough people leaving reviews?

A different problem, and the easier one to fix. The customers you served last year loved you — they were just never asked. I can run that for you too: a short, friendly ask sent to your past customers with a direct link to your Google review page. One tap and they're done.

Include it and I'll send them

Fair questions

Do I have to give you my Google password?

Never. You add me as a manager on your Google Business Profile — you can revoke it in two clicks — or you paste the replies in yourself from the file I send you. Either way works, and plenty of owners choose the second one.

Is replying to reviews actually allowed?

Yes — replying to real reviews is encouraged and Google says so plainly. What's against the rules is buying reviews, offering something in exchange for them, or only asking your happy customers. We do none of that.

What if a review is wrong or unfair?

We stay calm, we don't argue in public, and we never admit legal fault. We thank them, address the specific issue, and move the conversation offline to your phone. A well-handled bad review earns more trust than a perfect record does.

How fast is it?

Your audit comes back the same day you ask. Once you're on the monthly plan, new reviews get a drafted reply within 24 hours, and it goes up as soon as you approve it.
